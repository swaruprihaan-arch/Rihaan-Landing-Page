Security is the system, not a setting.

Principles

Six rules every run follows.

These are not options you switch on for sensitive workflows. They are how every workflow runs, for every agent, every time.

01

Isolated execution

Each step runs in a fresh sandbox with only the files, network routes and tools it declared. When the step ends, the sandbox is destroyed.

02

Least privilege

Credentials live in the vault, scoped to one workflow, injected at run time and never written to output or logs.

03

Approval gates

Any step can require a person. Reviewers see the exact diff or action, approve or reject in one click, and the decision is recorded.

04

Complete audit trail

Every prompt, response, file change and approval is captured with who, what and when, and can be exported to your monitoring stack.

05

Encrypted everywhere

Traffic between agents, the control plane and your systems is encrypted in transit. Stored transcripts and credentials are encrypted at rest.

06

Your data stays yours

Retention windows, hosting region and deletion are yours to set per workflow. Nothing you run is used to train anything.

Architecture

What sits between an agent and your systems.

Agents never talk to your repositories, tools or data directly. Every request passes through the control plane, where policy, the sandbox and the audit log do their work.

  1. Request

    A trigger starts a run: a schedule, a webhook, a pull request or a person. Rihaan resolves which workflow version to use and who is accountable for it.

  2. Policy check

    Before anything executes, each step's declared needs are checked against your policies. A step that asks for more than it is allowed never starts.

  3. Sandboxed execution

    The agent runs in an isolated environment with scoped credentials injected just in time. Its output is captured and reviewed, never trusted by default.

  4. Approval

    Steps marked for review pause the run. A person sees the change or action in full and decides. The run resumes or stops, and the decision is logged.

  5. Audit and delivery

    Results reach your systems through the connector, and the complete record of the run lands in the audit log, ready to export.
