How to Play

  • Click a tower card (or press 1-4 for the basic towers), then click an empty grid cell to build it.
  • Towers auto-fire at enemies within range, prioritizing whoever is furthest along the path.
  • Click a placed tower to select it — upgrade for a huge range boost plus more damage, or sell for a refund.
  • Click "Start Wave" (or Enter) to send the next wave. Enemies that reach the end cost you a life.
  • Browse 224 tower types across Basic, Elemental, Mechanical, Halo, Weapons and Vehicles — search or filter with the tabs.
  • Adopt Pets to guard your base — they fight enemies that get close, and some heal your lives over time.
  • Survive all 100 waves to win. The game gets harder every wave. Lose all lives and it's game over.
